# Cross functional coordination model

One named owner and one fixed cadence per function, not a general promise to communicate better. The real value this role adds is absorbing coordination that pod leads are currently doing themselves on top of building, so the model below is written around what each function actually needs, not a generic meeting cadence.

## HR, Elena Rodriguez

Keep the monthly cadence that already exists with David, but change the agenda from reactive firefighting to roadmap driven. The operations role becomes Elena's single point of contact for the technical roadmap, requirements, and interviewer availability, exactly what she asked for directly, replacing each pod lead reaching out to her separately. One honest limitation worth naming rather than assuming away: Elena has only 20 to 25 percent of her time available for DI and there is no dedicated technical recruiter, a real capacity constraint that a better handoff alone will not fully solve.

## Finance, James Okonkwo

A biweekly 30 minute sync tied to the hiring plan burn down, plus real time visibility through the vendor tracker itself rather than only the sync. James was explicit that he wants to stop being a historian who reports what already happened and become a partner who sees what is coming before the money is spent.

## Compliance, Sarah Kim

A monthly review, plus a fast versus slow lane split using the two gate criteria in the 90 day plan, cost and data sensitivity, both have to pass to skip a full review. Early engagement is built into the model itself, not just the cadence: a simple flag at intake, is this a new type of build or data use, triggers an early check in with Sarah rather than waiting for a formal submission at the end. She was explicit that a seat at the table during design, not a gate at launch, is what turns a three day review into a three week one.

## The vendor and contract process specifically

Today this involves four parties, the vendor, Legal, the shared part time security reviewer, and Sarah in Compliance, and Priya is the one manually coordinating all four herself. The operations role becomes the translation layer between the team's speed and the firm's process, doing that coordination so pod leads do not have to play project manager or recruiter on top of building. This is not a new layer of process on top of what exists, it replaces the current state, which is informal email with no ticketing or requisition flow.
