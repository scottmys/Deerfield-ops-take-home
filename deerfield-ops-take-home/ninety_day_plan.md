# 90 day plan

## First 30 days

Diligence completion, one weekly sync across both pods, one intake process for requests to HR, Finance, and Compliance, two or three visible quick wins.

## Days 31 to 60

The real systems: a shared prioritization method both pods use, a hiring plan tied to Finance's budget cycle, a fast lane versus slow lane for compliance and vendor requests.

## Days 61 to 90

Onboarding playbook ahead of the hiring wave, first retro, adjustments.

## What comes after day 90

Short note on sequencing for the next quarter, not a full plan.
